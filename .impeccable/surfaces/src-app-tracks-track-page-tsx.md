# AI + Data track surface brief

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** A role changes interview emphasis, not the shared technical foundation.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Technical Field Atlas: cool paper, dark ink, blueprint blue, restrained teal, index labels, ruled tables, and evidence-led language.
- **STORY:** Orient to the track → compare five role routes → open the priority field notes → continue through the complete topic manifest.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Track title, map action, and the opening of the five-route role index; no dashboard summary cards.
- **FORM:** Desktop-first technical manual table, first of the grounded structures; the user-pinned Technical Field Atlas remains authoritative over concept roll seed `7eb17694`.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Shipped pattern

- The role map uses the same ruled table grammar as the curriculum so it reads as an index rather than a separate product surface.
- Every role states focus, linked priority route, supporting depth, typical interview loop, and a concrete proof artifact.
- Desktop preserves five-column comparison; mobile retains role, priority route, and proof while hiding supporting detail available from the linked lessons.
- The complete 26-topic AI + Data manifest has its own heading and follows directly after the role routes.

## Quality bar

- Role routes are distinguishable without color.
- Priority topics are direct links, keyboard reachable, and descriptively named.
- ARIA table rows contain explicit column-header and cell roles.
- No horizontal page overflow at the supported mobile viewport.
- Automated WCAG A/AA checks pass on desktop and mobile.
